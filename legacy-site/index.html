FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

What is Texas Movement Media?

The signal layer of Texas Movement International. It documents the work, runs content systems, and distributes them with editorial discipline.

Where do I watch Shades of AI?

On YouTube via the Texas Movement Media channel, in the dedicated Shades of AI playlist.

Who is Media for?

The ecosystem lanes, collaborators, and audiences who want credible explanation of systems, performance, and economic leverage—not just entertainment.

Is Texas Movement Media a marketing agency?

No. It is an in-ecosystem media function focused on documentation and durable signal, not campaign churn or generic brand work.

How does Media connect to Social?

Media produces the signal; Social is where much of that signal shows up publicly and where people are routed from content into lanes.

How do I propose a collaboration or series?

Send a brief with scope, audience, and what the content needs to accomplish. Serious, systems-aligned collaborations are considered; hype is not.
