What is TMM

Not a podcast network. A media infrastructure.

Texas Movement Media is the content arm of Texas Movement International — producing original series, interviews, and documentary-style programming at the intersection of sport, culture, and systems thinking.

We don't cover the mainstream. We document what the mainstream ignores: diaspora audits, athletic excellence, performance science, and the founders building alternatives to broken institutions.
